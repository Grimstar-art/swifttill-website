Questions

Before you decide

Does SwiftTill work without internet?

Yes. Selling, stock, receipts and reports all work offline on the till. When the internet comes back, sales sync and backups catch up on their own. The M-Pesa prompt, eTIMS signing and WhatsApp messages need internet at the moment you use them.

What computer do I need?

A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC or laptop. A touchscreen is nice but not required. If you don't have hardware yet, tell us and we'll quote a till PC, receipt printer, cash drawer and barcode scanner.

How does M-Pesa work?

With your own Till (Buy Goods) or Paybill number connected, SwiftTill sends the payment prompt to the customer's phone and records the sale when they pay. You can also record a payment by its M-Pesa code, and a code that was already used is refused. On the Biashara and Matawi plans we connect it for you.

Is it ready for KRA eTIMS?

Yes. SwiftTill can sign receipts through eTIMS as you sell, and gives you a VAT report in Excel. You need your own KRA eTIMS registration; we help you connect it during set-up.

Is there a free trial?

We offer a free demo instead. We show you SwiftTill running a full demo shop, on a call or in person, and answer your questions with your own products in mind. Because every plan includes set-up and training, you start properly from day one.

What happens when my licence year ends?

The till reminds you 14 days before. You renew by M-Pesa from the till and it updates on its own. Your records stay on your computer either way.

Is my data safe? Who can see it?

Your sales and stock live on your own computer, with automatic backups so you don't lose them if a computer breaks. Each staff member has their own login, and sensitive actions like voids and refunds can need the owner's approval.

Can I bring my products in from Excel?

Yes. SwiftTill imports products from an Excel sheet. During set-up we load your product list for you.

Does it come in Kiswahili?

Yes. Staff can switch the screens to Kiswahili. Training and support are in English or Kiswahili.

Can I check on my shop when I'm not there?

Yes. You can have the day summary sent to your WhatsApp, and with several tills or branches everything syncs so you see the whole business.
